# Crumb redesign — concept field
Existing product: bakery storefront, six products, local cart and demo pickup checkout. Preserve all commercial demonstration boundaries.
Rejected incumbent: cream-and-green palette, Prata, right-photo split hero, identical cards, predictable alternating sections. This file records original concepts, not external site copies.

Seven viable grounded worlds, ordered by audience resonance:
1. Contemporary food still-life studio: cold photographic ground, massive type and isolated baked objects. Food becomes a tangible object, not lifestyle background.
2. Independent gastronomy magazine: full-bleed close crop, condensed headlines, changing photo/text density. Strong editorial reading rhythm.
3. Soft sculpture gallery: lilac and plum, lyrical serif, a loaf in a tall central plinth; slow luxurious product examination.
4. Bakery batch sheet: utilitarian blue-white printed order form, visible product quantities, tactile bread against precise grids.
5. Early-morning street kiosk: orange painted surfaces, typography as signage, visible daily selection at first viewport.
6. Table conversation: generous handwritten-scale headings, documentary contact sheets, reading path arranged around shared food.
7. Modern food campaign poster: cobalt and cool grey, huge soft geometric wordmark partially occluded by a photoreal loaf, asymmetric small copy, purchase controls in the scene. No split-hero structure.

Three browser prototypes to build, each genuinely distinct:
A / Campaign: giant cobalt crumb wordmark across cool-grey first screen, isolated bread cutting across its letters, fine purchase rail. Grotesk system; product-first high visual contrast.
B / Journal: immersive full-bleed macro bread photo with orange editorial margin, condensed Cyrillic headline anchored at foot, sequence of photographic spreads. Narrative-first.
C / Gallery: lavender exhibition canvas, small justified masthead, central isolated loaf as a still-life sculpture, high-contrast serif diagonal composition. Object-first quiet luxury.

Selection delegated by user. These are code prototypes requested by the user, not generated screenshot comps, and the user explicitly asks to choose without a decision gate. Compare actual browser renders before committing implementation.
