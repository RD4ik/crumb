# Storefront — complete redesign
Primary target: src/main.tsx. Related: src/style.css, src/fonts-v2.css.
Visitor mode: Persuade plus a working local shopping flow.
User rejects incumbent cream/green serif split hero and conventional cards. User explicitly requests three coded visual prototypes, browser screenshots, independent selection, complete new visual world and preserved bakery/cart/demo-order functionality. The user authorizes replacing DESIGN.md and its sidecar as part of the complete redesign, with no selection/approval gate. Preserve existing product IDs, prices, categories and order semantics.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A contemporary bread campaign that is immediately shoppable. A loaf crosses a monumental wordmark; no standard split hero or rectangular lifestyle photo.
OWN-WORLD: Cool photographic grey #e9edef, cobalt #203cc4, dark navy #182878, electric orange #ee693f for purchase controls and the full process section. Unbounded heavy lower-case brand with Golos Text for Cyrillic and utility. All product objects photographed at the same angle and light on genuine alpha; the bread supplies the texture.
STORY: Recognize the brand/product, add the featured loaf in place, browse an uneven open shelf, inspect ingredients, assemble a local pickup list. No payment or order transmission.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width oversized wordmark occupying the upper third, sourdough centrally crossing its lower edge; a compact Russian statement lower left, short factual prose lower right, functional quick-add rail aligned to lower-right edge. Mobile recomposes in one column with a readable wordmark, a large centered loaf, heading and purchase controls. Signature behavior: product/add control shows count immediately; native cart drawer retains context. One entrance sequence and responses to user action, no loops or parallax.
FORM: Direction A — Campaign, selected after browser comparison of all three prototypes; concept seed:f03192dbfaa405eb. User delegates final visual selection; comparison recorded in design/concepts/COMPARISON.md. This task builds from coded prototypes as explicitly requested, not an image comp.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
