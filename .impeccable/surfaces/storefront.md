# Storefront
Visitor mode: Persuade, with an operational local shopping flow.
Audience and product assumptions are recorded in PRODUCT.md. The user delegated direction and stack decisions, requested one implementation without variant rounds, and required browser verification.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A bakery counter at morning scale: tactile bread leads, and visitors can add the featured loaf directly. No ornamental app demo or fabricated commercial proof.
OWN-WORLD: Butter wrapping paper, dark bottle-green ink, Prata lettering, Manrope utility text. Round ingredient seal, thin rules, small rectangular controls; product photography gets the scale.
STORY: Meet the loaf, explore the actual catalog, understand ingredients, assemble a pickup list. Demo status is explicit at checkout and in the footer.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Oversized three-line Cyrillic headline at left; a close photographic loaf filling the right 53%; functional quick-add strip overlays its lower edge. Mobile restacks at true reading size. Signature interaction: a product moves into a persistent local basket with an immediate status announcement. Motion grammar: restrained image hover and one drawer entrance, no ambient loops.
FORM: Butter-paper bakery counter, candidate 7; seed:c5ca48ddddbc2194. User instruction overrides the interactive direction selection and multiple comp round; implementation is code-led in this session.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
